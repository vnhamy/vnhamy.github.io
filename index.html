The stories behind the wins

Two proudest battles, in detail.

Unilever Vietnam

Launch into the unknown - a new brand in a new category

New launching initiative in a brand-new category: taking Simple into Health & Beauty specialty channels, then extending into premium and international hypermarkets.

Pain points
  • Moving from mainstream Baby & Oral Care into mass-premium Skincare - no category experience to lean on.
  • Retailers demanded a premium margin that could either sink the new brand or blow the budget of the core business.
  • Hand-carry (grey-market) competition everywhere online, conflicting with official pricing and pulling shoppers away.
Ideas & execution
  • Restructure, not resist - a category-fit, separate deal to meet retailers while protecting the baseline.
  • Win on trust & value - build visionary pictures so retailers co-invested in unproven categories.
  • Partner to win - exclusive deals and strong collaboration to deliver the route-to-market plan.
  • Launch loud - secured key events so the launch landed with standout displays, not a quiet listing.
Key deliveries
  • Targeted the Health & Beauty specialty channel, then extended into premium international hypermarkets.
  • Premium assortment and pricing to beat grey-market products on value and trust.
  • Dedicated trade terms for NPD with clear, incremental investment.
  • Won exclusivity with hot deals & key-event activations; sampling and advisors to push trial.
  • Demand planning to secure a service level above 85%.

Mars Petcare

Winning Pet Food in Modern Trade - from volume-driven to value-built

Rather than fight a losing battle to cut trade terms, I re-engineered them and invested for future growth - making every dollar work.

Situation
  • An unhealthy revenue mix - most sales came from high-volume selling that flattered the short term but constantly conflicted with General Trade.
  • Budget trapped in deep discounts, with trading-term clauses that favored customers and were near-impossible to renegotiate down.
  • A narrow portfolio fighting itself - just 3 segments, so everyone chased the best deals on core SKUs, fueling channel conflict.
Must-win battles
  • Convert volume into value - wean the business off unhealthy high-volume sales; rebuild revenue on genuine, organic off-take.
  • Own Modern Trade leadership - Share of Shelf >55% and Assortment Share >60% to lead and be the fastest on the market.
Strategic initiatives
  • Rebuild healthy revenue - stopped high-volume leakage, regrew organic off-take with real promotions to protect long-term P&L.
  • Revamp trade terms - converted unconditional benefits into conditional, performance-linked investment tied to growth, visibility and availability.
  • Manage channel conflict - 80/20: secured ~80% core with quarterly key events, with floor prices pre-aligned across channels.
  • Grow the future 20% - shifted to premium lines and bigger value packs to drive consumption and reduce reliance on sensitive SKUs.
  • Empower team efforts - engaged the DKSH team via PG activations, bonus competitions and conferences to win free displays and expansion.
*Mars Petcare brands: Pedigree · Whiskas · Kitekat.
