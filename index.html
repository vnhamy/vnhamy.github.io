The stories behind the wins

Two proudest battles, in detail.

Unilever Vietnam

Winning the shelf - from scattered coverage to a structured assortment

As Customer Planning Manager I owned assortment, availability and share for every Unilever category across a wide, fragmented base of emerging-channel customers. I replaced ad-hoc, one-size-fits-all planning with a structured, data-led assortment framework - the right range in the right store, executed consistently through buyers and the field team.

Pain points
  • A broad category portfolio across a fragmented customer base made planning heavy and reactive - every account, even the key ones, handled ad-hoc rather than to a clear priority.
  • Availability (OSA) and Share of Shelf were mine to deliver but Operations' to execute - and both hinge on the assortment and merchandising agreed on the customer's side.
  • With no standard assortment logic, effort rarely converted into on-shelf results, and performance was hard to compare or improve across customers.
Ideas & execution
  • Prioritize the base - rank customers by contribution; manage top accounts individually and cluster the long tail by shared traits - channel, format, shopper and geography.
  • Define the core-of-the-core - build a tailored must-stock range for each account and cluster, driven by sales performance, shopper behaviour, demographics and geography.
  • Align with buyers - agree the new assortment and a store-by-store deployment plan with each buyer.
  • Execute with Operations - translate the agreed assortment-by-store into merchandising and availability actions with the field team.
  • Review and refine - track core-range performance monthly, prune underperformers and upgrade the list.
Key deliveries
  • A prioritized customer plan - key accounts managed individually, the long tail grouped into look-alike clusters.
  • A data-built core assortment (must-stock list) for every account and cluster.
  • Buyer-agreed listings translated into a store-by-store deployment plan.
  • A clear assortment-by-store brief for Operations to drive merchandising, visibility and availability.
  • A monthly review cycle to keep the core range sharp and performing.

Mars Petcare

Winning Pet Food in Modern Trade - from volume-driven to value-built

Rather than fight a losing battle to cut trade terms, I re-engineered them and invested for future growth - making every dollar work.

Must-win battles
01
Convert volume into value

Wean the business off unhealthy high-volume sales and rebuild revenue on genuine, organic off-take in the long-term.

02
Make every dollar work

Rather than fight a losing battle to cut trade terms and fight with customers, re-engineer them and invest for future growth.

03
Own Modern Trade leadership

Win in Share of Shelf >55% and Assortment Share >60% to establish leadership and be the fastest on the market.

04
Protect the core, build the future

Secure the core base by investing in the right places with the right activities, while reducing dependence on it through new initiatives.

Situation
  • An unhealthy revenue mix - most sales came from high-volume selling that flattered the short term but constantly conflicted with General Trade.
  • Budget trapped in deep discounts, with trading-term clauses that favored customers and were near-impossible to renegotiate down.
  • A narrow portfolio fighting itself - just 3 segments, so everyone chased the best deals on core SKUs, fueling channel conflict.
Strategic initiatives
  • Rebuild healthy revenue - stopped high-volume leakage, regrew organic off-take with real promotions to protect long-term P&L.
  • Revamp trade terms - converted unconditional benefits into conditional, performance-linked investment tied to growth, visibility and availability.
  • Manage channel conflict - 80/20: secured ~80% core with quarterly key events, with floor prices pre-aligned across channels.
  • Grow the future 20% - shifted to premium lines and bigger value packs to drive consumption and reduce reliance on sensitive SKUs.
  • Empower team efforts - engaged the DKSH team via PG activations, bonus competitions and conferences to win free displays and expansion.
